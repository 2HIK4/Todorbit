### Visual acceptance

The early Week preview concern was a display artifact. The original Android baseline PNG shows Month. The candidate PNG pixel data contains the Month button background and its complete text, and the candidate accessibility dump reports its name, clickability and in-bounds target. A screen-reader interaction check is separate from this capture review.

Upcoming reads better at both widths, and the Android Calendar has more room for the date and agenda at enlarged text. Shared widths, typography, separators and action placement still look like one product across the matched routes. On a full Today with several events, reaching the planned task group still takes vertical space. The current grouping is readable. The pre-existing numeric event range in category and project headings is less readable than the dates in their task rows. These are follow-up concerns outside the focused corrections. Light and dark stills support this judgment about treatment and hierarchy. Keyboard, touch and assistive behavior require their corresponding interaction checks.

No additional corrective pass is recommended from the inspected comparisons. This visual result supports Beta 28 preparation, subject to the user's dark-default decision and the separate runtime and release verification. The Windows traces support the narrower hour-rule paint change. They leave visible dense-calendar performance work because heavy raster scenes still miss the host's frame budget and the measured elapsed time rose in two transitions. See the [implementation and measurements](BETA_27_POLISH_IMPLEMENTATION.md).
