# Preliminary product-name review — 2026-10-19

This is public-source screening, not trademark clearance or legal advice. On 2026-10-20 the creator selected **Todorbit** for the public rebrand. Formal clearance remains open. The existing orbit logo is retained as the selected brand asset.

## TodoPilot risk

- [todopilot.app](https://www.todopilot.app/) advertises a task manager with priorities, lists, deadlines, and planned iOS/Android releases. Its owner identifies itself as DJUMP, MB in Lithuania.
- [todopilot.com](https://www.todopilot.com/) advertises a Windows personal task organizer named ToDoPilot, with day/week/month planning and a US price. This overlaps especially closely with this project's Windows use case.
- [SORA LLC's App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/todopilot/id6804931706) and an [independent macOS TodoPilot repository](https://github.com/blockdancez/TodoPilot) add further task-software uses. A [VS Code extension](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=green-spec.todo-pilot) uses “Todo Pilot” for task management.

Identical or near-identical wording on related software creates a practical risk of user confusion, mistaken downloads, search-result competition, and objections after launch. Different operating systems do not necessarily resolve confusion. The [USPTO explains](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) that similarity of marks and related goods/services both matter. **Recommendation: replace TodoPilot before a public release**, subject to the creator's choice and professional clearance of the chosen name. Keeping it would warrant a jurisdiction-specific clearance opinion first.

The [USPTO trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) and [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index) were identified as relevant public registers, but their interactive record searches did not yield verifiable result records through the available research tools. No “no registration found” claim is made. WIPO advises checking national and regional registers as well. A proper search should include exact, phonetic, and similar marks for downloadable software and task/productivity services in the intended markets, including the United States, EU, UK, and any planned distribution jurisdictions.
